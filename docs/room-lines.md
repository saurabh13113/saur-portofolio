# Room lines: in your own words

Everything the room and the avatar say, with a prompt for each. Write your version
after **Mine:** (leave it blank to keep the current line), save, and tell Claude
"room lines are filled in". The lines get copied into `components/room/roomObjects.js`
and `components/room/3d/Avatar3D.jsx`.

Keep lines short: they show in a small pixel bubble, so about 60 characters is the
most that reads well. Emoji are fine.
